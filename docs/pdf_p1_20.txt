Chapter 1  
Introduction  
  
The Hospital management involves the overall administration and coordination of hospital 
operations to ensure the efficient delivery of healthcare services, including the management of staff, 
facilities, patient records, and operational workflows. One important component of hospital management is 
the admission process, which focuses on registering patients, handling referrals, assigning appropriate 
departments or wards, and ensuring accurate documentation of patient information. Effective management 
of admission procedures is essential to reduce delays, minimize errors, and improve patient flow within the 
hospital. Studies have shown that standardizing and improving hospital admission and discharge processes 
can significantly enhance operational efficiency and reduce patient waiting time, thereby improving overall 
hospital performance and patient outcomes (Ortiga et al., 2012).  
Salaam Hospital, located in Blis Papandayan, Marawi City, currently operates without a formal  
digital system for managing patient records, admissions, and referrals. Because the hospital’s processes are 
largely manual, essential tasks such as tracking patient history, identifying which doctor or nurse assisted a 
patient, recording administered medi cations, and managing patient admissions are time -consuming and 
prone to human error. The lack of a central system hinders staff efficiency and negatively impacts patient 
care; therefore, a comprehensive hospital management and admission system is critically needed to support 
daily operations and improve the quality of services provided to patients.  
The absence of an effective system at Salaam Hospital has resulted in several operational 
challenges. With manual documentation, staff struggle to retrieve accurate historical patient information 
quickly, which can delay treatment decisions and complicate referrals among in -house specialists. In 
traditional paper-based record systems, healthcare providers often spend significant time searching for 
misfiled or missing patient folders, which negatively affects the timely delivery of healthcare services and 
the standard of care (Influence of Health Information Management Practices on the Quality of Healthcare, 
2023).

This study focuses on the development of a Hospital Management and Admission System aimed at  
modernizing hospital operations. The system is designed to handle various aspects of patient care, including 
medical history tracking, specialist referrals, treatment and medication documentation, and admission 
processes. By digitizing records and automati ng key functions, the system provides hospital staff with 
timely access to accurate patient information, facilitates communication among healthcare providers, and 
streamlines administrative tasks. Hospital Information Systems (HIS) have been shown to improve clinical 
workflows, reduce administrative delays, and enhance the quality of care by supporting efficient 
decisionmaking and effective management of patient data (Mohammadpour et al., 2021; Influence of Health 
Information Management Practices on the Quality of Healthcare, 2023).   
  
1.1 Project Context   
This capstone project entitled “Responsive Web-Based Patient Referral and Admission System for 
Salaam Hospital” focuses on addressing the inefficiencies in the hospital’s current manual processes for 
patient admission, medical record tracking, and internal  specialist referrals. At present, activities such as 
recording patient information, managing admission details, tracking medical history, and coordinating 
referrals among specialist doctors are conducted using handwritten forms, logbooks, or verbal 
communication. These manual processes are often time -consuming and prone to human error, leading to 
misplaced or incomplete records, delayed decision -making, miscommunication between staff, and 
difficulties in retrieving accurate patient histories. Such ineffici encies not only hinder smooth hospital 
operations but can also compromise patient safety and the overall quality of care (Shiferaw, Zewdu, & 
Sisay, 2023; Alemu, Mekonnen, & Getachew, 2023).  
To address these challenges, the project proposes the development of a computerized Hospital 
Management and Admission System that automates patient registration, admission tracking, medical history 
management, and internal referrals, while providing accessible digital records through both web and mobile 
platforms. By digitizing records and streamlining workflows, the system is expected to reduce

2.1 Review of Related Concepts   
2.1.1 Hospital Management Systems (HMS)  
Hospital Management Systems (HMS) are comprehensive software platforms that 
automate and streamline the operational, administrative, and clinical processes of healthcare 
facilities. HMS integrates multiple modules, including patient registration, appointment scheduling, 
billing, laboratory management, pharmacy, and human resource management. By centralizing all 
hospital operations, HMS reduces paperwork, prevents duplication of records, and improves 
communication between departments. the implementation of HMS enhances hospital efficiency by 
allowing staff to access patient and operational data in real time, enabling informed decision -
making and timely response to patient needs. For instance, a patient admitted through the HMS can 
have their medical history, lab results, and billing details immediately available to doctors, nurses, 
and administrative staff, which reduces delays and errors. Moreover, HMS supports reporting and 
analytics functions that help hospital management monitor resource utilization, track performance, 
and plan improvements in healthcare delivery. This system ultimately contributes to higher quality 
patient care and smoother hospital workflows.  
  
=== PAGE 13 ===

2.1.2 Patient Admission and Discharge Systems  
Patient Admission and Discharge Systems are specialized software solutions that manage 
the complete lifecycle of a patient’s stay in a healthcare facility, from admission to discharge. These 
systems handle patient intake, bed allocation, recording of medic al histories, generation of 
discharge summaries, and coordination with billing departments. By automating these processes, 
hospitals can reduce patient waiting times, minimize errors in documentation, and improve 
workflow efficiency among healthcare staff.  According to research, such systems enhance 
operational management by providing real -time updates on bed availability, patient status, and 
required treatments. For example, when a patient is admitted, the system can automatically notify 
nurses, doctors, and lab personnel about the patient’s condition and scheduled tests, ensuring timely 
care. Additionally, centralized recordkeeping ensures that all patient information is securely stored 
and easily accessible for future reference, supporting continuity of c are and informed 
decisionmaking.  
  
2.1.3 Integration of EMR/EHR with Hospital Management Systems  
The integration of Electronic Medical Records (EMR) and Electronic Health Records 
(EHR) with Hospital Management Systems (HMS) allows hospitals to create a seamless, 
centralized digital platform that connects administrative and clinical processes. This int egration 
enables real -time synchronization of medical histories, diagnostic reports, treatment plans, and 
billing information across departments. Studies indicate that such integration improves operational 
efficiency, reduces redundant testing, and enhances continuity of care by ensuring that all healthcare 
providers have access to accurate and up-to-date patient information. For example, when a patient 
is transferred from one department to another, doctors and nurses can immediately access previous 
test re sults, medications, and referral notes, minimizing delays and potential errors. Moreover,

integrating EMR/EHR with HMS supports advanced analytics, enabling hospitals to monitor 
performance, plan resource allocation, and improve service delivery.  
  
2.1.4 Digital Admission Management Systems in Hospital Operations   
Digital admission management systems in hospitals provide a centralized electronic 
platform that streamlines patient intake and replaces traditional manual or paper -based admission 
workflows. These systems often include features such as online pre-registration, real-time tracking 
of patient admissions, and integrated data capture at the point of service, allowing administrative 
and clinical staff to manage admissions efficiently. Research indicates that digital admission 
systems improve operational efficien cy, reduce patient waiting times, and enhance coordination 
among registration, clinical, and administrative units.   
Hospitals implementing such systems report faster patient turnaround, while patients and 
families benefit from greater convenience and reduced waiting. Electronic admission platforms also 
minimize administrative workload, decrease documentation errors, and provide real-time access to 
bed availability, admission progress, and patient flow. For example, when a patient is admitted 
through the digital system, all relevant staff can immediately access their registration details and 
medical information, ensuring smooth transitions between departments. Integrating digital 
admission management into the proposed Hospital Management and Admission System for Salaam 
Hospital can therefore optimize patient flow, improve data accuracy, and support higher -quality 
service delivery.

2.3.2 CNR Software Systems  
The CNR Software Systems developed the Appoluz Hospital Information System (HIS) 
and Electronic Medical Records (EMR) to automate hospital operations and improve the efficiency 
of patient care in Philippine health facilities. Its primary purpose is to stre amline workflows, 
including patient registration, admissions, medical records management, laboratory and radiology 
processes, pharmacy inventory, and billing, thereby reducing manual paperwork and minimizing 
errors. The system was designed as a comprehensi ve digital platform that integrates all major 
hospital departments and supports real -time data management. Patients’ medical information is 
digitized through EMR, allowing doctors and nurses to access historical records, vital signs, 
diagnoses, and e-prescriptions easily. The system also supports ICD-10 coding, automated billing, 
and PhilHealth or HMO processing to ensure regulatory compliance and accurate documentation. 
CNR Software Systems developed the platform locally in the Philippines, focusing on costeffective 
solutions accessible to hospitals of various sizes. The Appoluz HIS incorporates features such as 
smart pharmacy inventory, laboratory and radiology result management, patient queuing, and 
financial reporting modules. It is DOH -accredited, which ensures that it meets national standards 
for e-health systems. Many hospitals and clinics in the Philippines, including Clinica  
Antipolo, Soriano -Leyble Medical and Maternity Hospital, St. Therese Hospital, San Isidro 
Hospital, and Taytay Maternity and Child Care Center, have implemented the system. Reported 
benefits include improved workflow efficiency, reduced paperwork, better c oordination among 
departments, accurate and faster billing, and streamlined access to patient information. The system 
allows hospital staff to monitor patient progress, track laboratory and pharmacy usage, and maintain 
a centralized database for easier dec ision-making. While there is no formal peer -reviewed study 
quantifying its impact, the adoption of Appoluz HIS has reportedly enhanced operational efficiency, 
improved documentation, and supported hospitals in providing more organized and reliable patient 
care.
